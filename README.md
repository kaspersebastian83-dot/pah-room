# PAH Room

A walk-in virtual reality lesson on polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), the substances that form whenever something burns without enough air. It is made for people who have heard the term but have no chemistry background, such as new colleagues in fire-damage assessment.

It is a single web page. In a VR headset you stand next to the molecules, build them with your hands, and follow one of them into the body. On an ordinary screen it works as a flat preview with mouse and keyboard.

## Opening it

**In a Meta Quest headset:** open the Quest's Browser app, go to this site's address, and press **Enter VR**. Nothing has to be installed.

**On a computer or phone:** open the same address. You get the flat view, with the same tour and the same scenes.

## What is in it

**Part one, the tour (about ten minutes)**

- A dive into a grain of soot, from 180,000× to 600,000,000× magnification.
- Building molecules ring by ring: naphthalene, anthracene, phenanthrene and benzo[a]pyrene.
- Touching a molecule, plucking its atoms, and seeing how it moves at room temperature and in a flame.
- Stepping inside benzo[a]pyrene at 1 Å = 1 m.

**Part two, into the body**

- Through a cell's outer membrane.
- Two enzymes turn benzo[a]pyrene into the reactive diol epoxide, step by step.
- The molecule slides into DNA, bonds to it, and causes a copying error.
- One damaged cell among many grows into a tumour.

Outside the tours, all eight molecules can be viewed, built and handled freely.

## Controls in the headset

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Grab and turn the molecule | Grip |
| Stretch or shrink it | Both grips |
| Push an atom | Touch it with the probe tip or a fingertip |
| Pluck an atom | Trigger on the atom, drag, release |
| Take or place a ring when building | Trigger |
| Pick something on the shelf | Point and trigger |
| Move while inside a molecule | Left stick glides, right stick turns and rises |

## What is real and what is simplified

**Computed**

- The shapes of the eight PAHs: density functional theory, B3LYP/6-31G(d), gas phase.
- The shapes of the three metabolites of benzo[a]pyrene, and all vibrations: GFN2-xTB.
- The final metabolite is the (7R,8S,9S,10R) diol epoxide.
- Thermal motion uses the quantum harmonic amplitude of every vibrational mode at the chosen temperature, slowed about 15 trillion times.

**Schematic**

- DNA is shown as coloured blocks and beads with real proportions, not as individual atoms.
- The enzymes are lumpy shapes at roughly true size, not real protein structures.
- The tumour scene, its cell count and its year counter illustrate the idea and are not taken from data.
- The explanations of where each substance turns up and what it does are written in plain language and leave out detail.

This is teaching material. It is not medical advice and not a risk assessment.

## Privacy

The page loads nothing from other servers and sends nothing anywhere. It remembers which molecules you have built, and whether you have finished the tour, in your own browser's storage on your own device.
